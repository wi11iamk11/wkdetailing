📚 Canvas Organizer

Pulls your courses and assignments out of Canvas and turns them into one sorted to-do list. Everything stays on this computer.

How do I get a token?
  1. Open Canvas and go to Account → Settings.
  2. Scroll to Approved Integrations and click + New Access Token.
  3. Name it "Canvas Organizer", leave the expiry blank, and click Generate Token.
  4. Copy the token and paste it above — Canvas only shows it once.
